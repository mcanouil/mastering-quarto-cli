## Session 4: Formats & Projects (14:00 - 14:55 CEST)

- **Exploring Formats**
  - Overview of the diverse output formats available in Quarto: HTML, Typsts, Reveal.js, PDF (LaTeX), MS Word, websites, blogs, books, and more.
  - Discussion on the benefits and limitations of each format.
- **Project Structures**
  - Analyse different project frameworks: single documents versus multi‑document projects such as websites or books.
- **Hands‑On Exercise**
  - Participants experiment with converting a sample document into multiple formats and setting up projects accordingly.
